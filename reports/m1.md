# M1: conditional circuit-construction pilot

**Status: complete.** All six authorized searches and their assessments completed
on 2026-09-11 UTC (2026-09-12 Europe/Berlin). There were no pilot execution failures,
no reruns for weak results, and no budget extensions. The larger study was not run.

The executable reconstruction passes 24 focused tests. At 384 proposals per run,
GP's mean interpolation loss was **0.392649**, versus **0.341745** for random search.
The mean paired GP-minus-random difference was **+0.050905**; smaller loss is better.
Only seed 202 favored GP on interpolation. No selected circuit met the frozen
engineering success criterion on any assessed requirement. These observations
separate correct mechanisms, successful execution, fitness improvement, and filter
synthesis: the first two were achieved; the pilot does not support reliable
generalized filter synthesis or a held-out H1 advantage.

## Implementation and source fidelity

The package in [src/evolved_circuits](../src/evolved_circuits) implements DEAP typed
construction trees, a lazy interpreter, an L/C graph representation and SPICE-style
netlist export, a complex nodal AC solver, training-only fitness, GP, independent
random search, compact continuation checkpoints, post-search assessment, and
plots from saved data. The [mechanism note](../docs/MECHANISM.md) documents every
operator and the short source-to-implementation correspondence.

One genotype develops both original embryo segments. Requirements enter only
through typed numerical terminals. The selected branch alone allocates circuit
elements. Predicate and value expression types cannot cross through variation.
There are no filter macros, manually seeded solutions, template dispatch, gradient
tuning, or model calls in the experiment. Hand-written filters appear only in
correctness fixtures. All six reported champions were selected from actual search
proposals using training loss, mean active-component count, then tree-node count.

The initial depth prior, expression/value mapping, and restricted graph grammar
are reconstruction choices. A pre-pilot read-only review caught that an initially
drafted branch depth 0..4 could produce 161 nodes; it was fixed at 0..3, yielding at
most 65 initial nodes. The protocol left this prior constant unspecified. No
training/assessment split, scoring rule, seed, population, or proposal budget was
changed. Two other pre-pilot review findings were repaired: retaining the process
lock across launcher death and accurately reporting worker startup failures.
Both read-only reviewers then found no blocking issue. Reviewers did not write
code, launch experiments, or run tests; the primary agent performed those actions.

## Configuration and execution

The exact [configuration](../configs/m1.json) is also embedded in the
[manifest](../artifacts/m1/manifest.json). Source/load are 1 V AC and 1 kΩ/1 kΩ,
with fixed through-gain normalization 0.5. Training covers six requirements at
b = 1, 10, 100 kHz in both orientations, using 81 log frequencies plus exact
boundaries. Post-search evaluation covers dense training (6), interpolation (12),
and extrapolation (4) at 401 log frequencies plus exact boundaries. Each band has
weight 0.5; invalid cases score 1e9 and squared residuals clip at 1e6. Success
requires every passband error ≤0.10 and every stopband amplitude ≤0.10.

Seeds were 101, 202, 303. Population was 48; each run used the initial 48 proposals
and seven 48-offspring batches, totaling 384. GP used size-3 tournaments, crossover
0.70, mutation 0.25 (equal subtree/constant probabilities), reproduction 0.05,
and two parent elites. Independent random search used the identical initial prior.
Within-pair order was randomized once with seed 1701; it happened to give GP then
random in all three pairs. Each method reinitialized its own RNG with the paired
search seed, producing identical first populations. Execution order was not
re-randomized to obtain a more balanced-looking outcome.

The following GP/303 champion is selected **post hoc for mechanism illustration**,
because its nine-node program includes an executed conditional. It remains the
same champion selected on training loss before assessment:

```text
Embryo(L(vF1), If(pF2, L(2.9234448256610976), C(vF1)))
```

For the saved lowpass requirement (1000,2000), `pF2 = log10(2) > 0`, and the
second branch creates L. For highpass (2000,1000), `pF2 = 0`, and it creates C.
The two labeled graphs have respectively two inductors and one inductor plus one
capacitor; their component-type multiplicities prove a graph difference independent
of numeric values or arbitrary node names. The condition tests whether F2 exceeds
1 kHz. At larger requirements both orientations choose L, explaining the program's
poor highpass generalization. No structural-difference bonus was used in fitness.

## Limitations and conclusion

Three seeds and 384 proposals are a small exploration of a restricted ideal-passive
language. The value prior and piecewise protected arithmetic influence what is easy
to find. Branch thresholds and numerical mappings can fit a sparse requirement
grid without implementing a general mode switch. Invalid floating islands are
penalized; isolated valid zero-output circuits remain valid but incur loss.
Analytic tests do not establish hardware accuracy or historical reproduction,
and no ngspice agreement was measured. The engineering threshold is much weaker
than the historical study's goal and still was not met.

M1 delivered a working, inspectable developmental GP pilot and a negative mean
held-out H1 comparison at its declared budget. There is no remaining executable
M1 work or experiment blocker. Future method changes made after these observations
must treat this holdout as developmental evidence and freeze a fresh assessment
set for confirmatory work. No later study has been started. The outer launcher
owns commit, push and remote-SHA verification; this report makes no publication
claim.
